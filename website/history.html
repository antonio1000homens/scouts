History

Our Story

2nd Tolworth Scout Group was founded in 1933. The group has been based at The Den in Princes Avenue since 1957.

Generations of young people have camped, climbed, cooked, and contributed under our purple and green necker. The group has changed with the times, but the core idea has stayed the same: give young people challenge, friendship, and the confidence to do more than they thought possible.

Milestones

  • 1933: 2nd Tolworth Scout Group was founded.
  • 1957: the group moved to The Den in Princes Avenue.

We are proud of the heritage that has shaped the group and determined to carry it forward. If you have old photographs, logbooks, or memories of 2nd Tolworth, we would love to add them to our archive. Please reach out via the contact page.
