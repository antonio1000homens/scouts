Scouts (10½–14 years)

Our Scout Troop meets on Fridays, 7:00–9:00 pm and is where the adventure really ramps up. Scouts take ownership of their programme, learn advanced skills, and head further afield—camping under canvas, mountain biking in Surrey Hills, and hitting the water in kayaks.

What to Expect

  • Leadership opportunities: Patrol Leaders and Assistants run activities with leader support.
  • Expeditions: weekend hikes, survival camps, and county competitions.
  • Skills for life: first aid, navigation, pioneering, digital skills, and citizenship.
  • International Scouting: jamborees, camps abroad, and cultural exchange projects.

Participation, resilience, and teamwork are at the heart of everything we do. We encourage Scouts to challenge themselves, speak up for their ideas, and support each other.
