Volunteering

Volunteer with Us

Our volunteers are the beating heart of 2nd Tolworth. Whether you can spare an hour a month or a full evening each week, there is a role for you.

Every skill, from admin wizardry to camping know-how, helps create life-changing experiences for our young people. Training and support are provided, and there is room for regular leaders, occasional helpers, and people who want to contribute a specific skill.

Ways to Help

  • Section Leadership: join the Beaver, Cub, or Scout team, help plan programmes, and run activities.
  • Parent Rota: support one meeting per term, help with games, craft, and tuck.
  • Group Executive: guide strategy, budgets, fundraising, and governance.
  • Skills on demand: share a one-off skill session, from first aid and coding to bushcraft and music.

Volunteering also looks fantastic on a CV and is a practical way to build confidence, leadership, and teamwork. Ready to get involved? Contact our Group Scout Leader via gsl@2ndtolworthscouts.org.uk or use the contact form.

Why people join

Flexible commitment

Help weekly, monthly, or for one-off events and trips.

Training included

You do not need previous Scouting experience to make a strong contribution.

Visible impact

Your time directly supports adventure, skills, and confidence for local young people.
