Volunteer with Us

Our volunteers are the beating heart of 2nd Tolworth. Whether you can spare an hour a month or a full evening each week, there is a role for you. Every skill—from admin wizardry to camping know-how—helps create life-changing experiences for our young people.

Ways to Help

  • Section Leadership: join the Beaver, Cub, or Scout team, help plan programmes, and run activities.
  • Parent Rota: support one meeting per term, help with games, craft, and tuck.
  • Group Executive: guide strategy, budgets, fundraising, and governance.
  • Skills on demand: share a one-off skill session—first aid, coding, bushcraft, music… we love experts!

Training and support are provided, and volunteering looks fantastic on a CV. Ready to get involved? Contact our Group Scout Leader via gsl@2ndtolworthscouts.org.uk or use the contact form.
