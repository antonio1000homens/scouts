About 2nd Tolworth Scout Group

2nd Tolworth Scout Group was founded in 1933 and has been based at The Den in Princes Avenue since 1957. We welcome young people from all genders, races, backgrounds, and abilities.

Scouting gives young people opportunities to try activities, work with others, and build skills for life. Our sections include Beavers and Cubs; contact us to register interest in Scouts.

Our group is run by volunteers. If you would like to help, please get in touch through the contact form.
