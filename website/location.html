Finding the Den

The 2nd Tolworth Scout Den is located on Hamilton Avenue, Tolworth, Surbiton KT6 7PT. We are a short walk from Tolworth Broadway and easily accessible from the A3. On meeting nights, please park considerately on surrounding roads.

Travel Tips

  • By car: enter KT6 7PT into your satnav. There is no on-site parking, but plenty of street parking on Hamilton Avenue and neighbouring roads.
  • By public transport: Tolworth railway station is a 10-minute walk. Bus routes 406, 418, and K2 stop nearby.
  • By bike or on foot: we have bike racks and a safe pathway into the Den—perfect for local journeys.

What3Words

For pinpoint accuracy, use ///scout.torch.hill (example) when navigating with the what3words app.
