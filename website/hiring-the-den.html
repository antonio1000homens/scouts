Hire Our Den

Need a flexible space for a community meeting, birthday party, or training session? The 2nd Tolworth Scout Den is available to hire when it is not being used for Scouting. Situated on Hamilton Avenue, the Den offers a large hall, kitchen facilities, a secure outdoor area, and easy access from Tolworth and Surbiton.

Facilities

  • Large main hall with tables, chairs, and projection screen.
  • Fully equipped kitchen with serving hatch.
  • Secure garden space ideal for games and BBQs.
  • Accessible toilets, including baby-changing facilities.
  • On-site storage for regular user groups.

Rates & Availability

Standard hire is £30 per hour (£80 for a three-hour party booking). Discounts are available for long-term community partners. The hall is usually available on weekday mornings and afternoons, plus selected weekends.

To check dates, request a viewing, or discuss regular hire, please email hallhire@2ndtolworthscouts.org.uk or use the contact form.
