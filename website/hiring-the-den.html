Hire Our Den

Need a flexible space for a community meeting, birthday party, or training session? The 2nd Tolworth Scout Den is available to hire when it is not being used for Scouting. Situated on Hamilton Avenue, the Den offers a large hall, kitchen facilities, a secure outdoor area, and easy access from Tolworth and Surbiton.

Why Hire Our Den

🏠

Facilities

Large main hall with tables, chairs, projection screen, a fully equipped kitchen, accessible toilets, and a secure outdoor area for games or BBQs.

💷

Rates & Availability

Standard hire is £30 per hour or £80 for a three-hour party booking. The Den is usually available on weekday mornings, afternoons, and selected weekends.

📍

Location

Based on Hamilton Avenue with easy access from Tolworth and Surbiton, the Den works well for community groups, parties, meetings, and training sessions.
