Beaver Scouts (6–8 years)

Beavers is the first step on the Scouting adventure. Our Beaver Scout Colony meets on Mondays from 5:30 to 6:45 pm at The Den. Beavers are for young people aged 6 to 8. Contact us to ask about joining or arrange a free taster session.

What Beavers Get Up To

  • Mini adventures: woodland walks, pond dipping, den building, and mini hikes.
  • Creative challenges: cooking, STEM experiments, and themed badge nights.
  • Community action: collecting for food banks and helping local charities.
  • Sleepovers: their first nights away from home with friends and leaders.

For more information or to arrange a free taster session, please contact us.
