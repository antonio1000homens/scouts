Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information 2nd Tolworth Scout Group collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Who We Are

2nd Tolworth Scout Group is a local Scout Group based in Tolworth, Surbiton. This website is provided to share information about our sections, activities, volunteering, and hall hire.

Information We Collect

If you contact us through the website, we collect the name, email address, and message you submit. We also process a Cloudflare Turnstile CAPTCHA token to help protect the form from spam and abuse.

How We Use It

We use your enquiry details only to review your message, respond to your question, and manage follow-up related to joining, volunteering, hall hire, or general group administration.

How The Form Works

Website enquiries are submitted to our Cloudflare Worker and then forwarded to our notification workflow so the appropriate volunteers can respond. We do not use the public website as a mailing-list signup system.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The public pages of this website do not intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. Essential technical services used to operate the site, including Cloudflare Turnstile on the contact form, may use browser storage or similar technologies as part of security and anti-abuse checks.

Third-Party Services

When you use the contact form, your submission is processed through Cloudflare for form handling and CAPTCHA verification, and then forwarded through IFTTT so the message reaches the relevant group volunteers. Those services process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep website enquiries only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage safeguarding or administrative follow-up, and maintain appropriate records. We do not use website enquiries for unrelated marketing.

Your Rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or request that we correct or delete it where appropriate. If you would like to make a privacy-related request, please use our contact form and describe your request clearly.

External Links

This website may link to other websites for useful information. Once you leave our website, the privacy practices of those external sites are controlled by the organisations that run them.

Updates To This Policy

We may update this page from time to time to reflect changes to the website or to our data-handling practices. The latest version published on this page is the version that applies.
