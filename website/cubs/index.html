Cub Scouts (8–10½ years)

Our Cub Scout Pack meets on Wednesdays from 6:30 to 8:00 pm. Most meetings take place at The Den. Cubs are for young people aged 8 to 10½. Contact us to ask about joining or arrange a free taster session.

Programme Highlights

  • Adventure skills: map reading, pioneering, problem solving, and team challenges.
  • Badges galore: from Astronomer to Chef, there’s a badge for every interest.
  • Nights away: weekend camps, sleepovers at the Den, and joint events with other packs.
  • Skills for life: learning to cook, communicate, and care for the environment.

For more information or to arrange a free taster session, please contact us.
