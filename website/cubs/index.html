Cub Scouts (8–10½ years)

Our Cub Pack meets on Wednesday evenings, 6:30–8:00 pm. Cubs build on Beaver foundations with bigger adventures, responsibility, and more chances to lead. The programme balances practical skills, community service, outdoor exploration, and plenty of fun.

Programme Highlights

  • Adventure skills: map reading, pioneering, problem solving, and team challenges.
  • Badges galore: from Astronomer to Chef, there’s a badge for every interest.
  • Nights away: weekend camps, sleepovers at the Den, and joint events with other packs.
  • Skills for life: learning to cook, communicate, and care for the environment.

Leaders regularly share programme outlines via email so you know what to expect each week. Parents can view badge progress in Online Scout Manager and support activities at home.
