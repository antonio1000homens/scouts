Cub Badges

Cubs love collecting badges that reflect their growth. From backwoods cooking to community service, our Pack works on challenge and activity badges all year round.

Badge requirements are shared in Online Scout Manager. If a Cub completes something outside meetings—music exams, sports, or creative achievements—let us know so we can celebrate it together.
