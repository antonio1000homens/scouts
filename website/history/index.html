Our Story

Scouting arrived in Tolworth not long after Baden-Powell launched the movement in 1907. 2nd Tolworth Scout Group was formally opened in the early 1930s and has been an active part of the local community ever since. Generations of young people have camped, climbed, cooked, and contributed under our purple and green necker.

Milestones

  • 1930s: the first troop meets in Tolworth, holding activities in borrowed church halls and fields on the edges of town.
  • 1954: the group secures the current Den on Hamilton Avenue, giving us a dedicated base for weekly meetings.
  • 1970s–1990s: expansion to include Beaver Scouts and a thriving Cub Pack, with regular international expeditions.
  • 2007: centenary celebrations mark 100 years of global Scouting with a district camp and street parade.
  • Today: more than 100 young people and adult volunteers keep the flame alive with a packed, modern programme.

We are proud of the heritage that has shaped the group and determined to carry it forward. If you have old photographs, logbooks, or memories of 2nd Tolworth, we would love to add them to our archive. Please reach out via the contact page.
