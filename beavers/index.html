Beaver Scouts (6–8 years)

Beavers is the first step on the Scouting adventure. Our Beaver Colony meets every Thursday, 6:00–7:00 pm during term time. Sessions are fun-filled, fast-paced, and packed with games, crafts, stories, and outdoor discovery.

What Beavers Get Up To

  • Mini adventures: woodland walks, pond dipping, den building, and mini hikes.
  • Creative challenges: cooking, STEM experiments, and themed badge nights.
  • Community action: collecting for food banks and helping local charities.
  • Sleepovers: their first nights away from home with friends and leaders.

We maintain a waiting list for Beavers, so please register early. Parents are encouraged to lend a hand—just once a term makes a huge difference.
