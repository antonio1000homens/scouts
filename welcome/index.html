Welcome to 2nd Tolworth

We are excited that you are interested in being part of 2nd Tolworth Scout Group. Our young people, volunteers, and families work together to make sure every meeting sparks imagination, builds confidence, and delivers the skills for life that Scouting is famous for.

Across Beavers, Cubs, and Scouts we run weekly programmes that blend outdoor adventures, team challenges, creativity, and community service. We are proud to be inclusive—everyone is welcome, and we’ll work with you to make sure your young person feels at home from the very first night.

Getting Started

  • Come along for a taster evening: let your young person see what we do and meet the leadership team.
  • Complete the joining form: we’ll guide you through the quick membership steps and make sure uniform, key dates, and subs are all clear.
  • Stay in the loop: look out for our parent emails, programme updates, and sign-up links for events and camps.

If you have any questions or would like to talk to one of the leaders before visiting, drop us a line via the contact page—we would love to hear from you.
