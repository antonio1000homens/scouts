Nights Away & Camping

Whether camping, hostelling, or sleepovers, nights away form an integral part of Scouts.

Find your adventure →

Build independence outdoors: young people learn practical camping skills, teamwork, and confidence away from home.

Look forward to memorable trips: from local sleepovers to bigger camps, nights away are often the highlights of the year.

Activities

From sports and games to crafts and outdoor adventures, there's something for everyone.

Discover activities →

Every term has variety: meetings can include challenges, problem solving, creative projects, and plenty of outdoor fun.

Skills grow naturally: young people try new things, work in teams, and build resilience without it feeling like school.

Badges & Awards

Master something you love, or try something new. Work towards challenge badges and awards.

Explore badges →

Recognition for every interest: badges reward progress in everything from outdoor skills to creativity, teamwork, and community action.

A clear sense of achievement: awards help young people set goals and celebrate the confidence they gain along the way.
