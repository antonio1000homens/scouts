About Us

We are a vibrant and active Scout Group providing fun, adventure, and skills for life to young people in our community. Our group has been serving the area for many years, offering activities for children and young people aged 6 to 18.

Scouting helps young people develop leadership skills, make new friends, and experience outdoor adventures in a safe and supportive environment.
