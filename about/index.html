About Us

Thank you for joining our Group. The purpose of this Welcome Pack is to give you a general introduction and information about our modern and active movement which is relevant to the needs of our young people today.

It gives a clear idea of the principles on which Scouting is based and helps everyone to see how they can make the best use of their interests and abilities.

The aim of the Scout Association is to promote the development of young people in achieving their full physical, intellectual, social and spiritual potential, as individuals, as responsible citizens, and as members of their local, national and international communities.

The Law and Promise is what makes Scouting different from any other youth organisation in the World.

We also have a uniform that is always worn on any Scouting activity unless told not to.

The one event in the year that you as parents and members REQUIRED to attend is the Annual General Meeting and Presentation Awards ceremonies.

The Scout Association takes pride in its safety record. Guidance is provided to Leaders through their District and County support. Safety issues are always covered in Leader training.

The young person who feels that their family and friends support them and are interested in what they do in Scouting will always achieve more.

There are many ways of providing support. You may have skills which could be useful either directly to the Colony/Pack/Troop or to the whole Group.

Scout Groups are run entirely by Volunteers and self financing. Our Group needs your support to help raise funds for Equipment and the general running costs of our own Headquarters for your child to be able to get the very best from their scouting.
